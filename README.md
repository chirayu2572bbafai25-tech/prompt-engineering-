Prompt Engineering Project

1. This project demonstrates the fundamentals and practical applications of Prompt Engineering.
2. It includes three levels of prompts: Basic, Advanced, and Business Prompts.
3. Basic prompts are designed for simple AI queries and everyday tasks.
4. Advanced prompts use context, constraints, roles, and structured instructions.
5. Business prompts focus on real-world organizational and decision-making use cases.
6. Prompts are designed to improve the accuracy, relevance, and consistency of AI responses.
7. The project demonstrates techniques such as role prompting, few-shot prompting, and structured prompting.
8. Each prompt is tested and refined to achieve better AI-generated outputs.
9. The project highlights how effective prompts can improve productivity and automation.
10. The overall goal is to develop practical prompt-engineering skills for professional AI applications.
